Frequently asked questions

How do I connect Happy Health to Apple Health?

In the app, go to Settings → Apple Health → “Connect to Apple Health”. If you declined access earlier, allow it in the Health app: profile picture at the top right → Apps → Happy Health.

My data doesn’t show up on my other device.

Syncing works through your private iCloud. Check that you’re signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices and that iCloud Drive is turned on. The first sync can take a few minutes, especially on Wi-Fi with many photos.

How do I back up my data?

Besides iCloud, you can export a backup file under Settings → Data & iCloud and restore it later, for example when switching devices.

Apple Watch doesn’t start along.

The watch app starts automatically when you begin a workout on iPhone. Happy Health needs to be installed on the watch (Watch app on iPhone → Available Apps), Apple Health needs to be connected, and “Start Apple Watch too” needs to be on under Settings → Live Workout.

Does Happy Health cost anything?

No. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Everything stays on your device and in your private iCloud. Read more in the privacy policy.
