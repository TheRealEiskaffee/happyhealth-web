Happy Health

Training that makes you happy.

Happy Health logs your strength training, shows you how you train, and celebrates your progress. Free, no ads, no subscription, no account.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone with iOS 26, with an Apple Watch app.

New in version 1.1

  • Heart rate per set: with Apple Watch you see the average and peak of every set and how your heart rate recovers during rest.
  • Warm-up sets per exercise, with the weights worked out for you.
  • Workout history with all your workouts by month.
  • Body: your weight with a 7-day average and new charts for weight and measurements.
  • Reached your goal weight? Confetti, and you decide whether to set a new goal or maintain your weight.

What to expect

  • Training plans with 90+ exercises, supersets, and warm-up sets
  • Live workouts with rest timer, Dynamic Island, and Apple Watch
  • Wins get celebrated, setbacks are never judged
  • Weight trend instead of daily ups and downs, body measurements, and progress photos
  • Insights on intensity, rests, heart rate, and training time
  • Apple Health, widgets, Siri, and Shortcuts
